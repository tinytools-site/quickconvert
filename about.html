About QuickConvert

Converters and utilities that respect your time and your privacy.

QuickConvert started with a simple frustration: most online converters are slow, covered in pop-ups, and demand an account before showing a single result. We build the opposite — lightweight tools that load instantly, give you the answer, and ask for nothing in return.

What we offer

Ten carefully built utilities for converting and transforming everyday data: live currency conversion, time zone conversion with world clocks, a JSON formatter and validator, Base64 and URL encoders, a HEX/RGB/HSL color converter, a QR code generator, text-to-binary conversion, UNIX timestamp conversion and a lorem ipsum generator. Nearly every tool runs entirely in your browser.

Our principles

  • Free forever: no paywalls, no premium tiers, no sign-ups.
  • Private: your inputs are processed locally and never sent to our servers.
  • Fast: plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript — no heavy frameworks.
  • Accurate: live exchange rates, real time-zone data and verified conversion logic.

How we stay free

QuickConvert is supported by advertising. This keeps every tool free while letting us add new ones. If you have a suggestion for a tool you would like to see, please contact us.
