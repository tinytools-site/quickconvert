Frequently asked questions

What is QuickConvert?

QuickConvert is a free collection of online converters and everyday web utilities — currency, time zones, JSON, Base64, colors, QR codes and more. Everything runs in your browser with no sign-up.

Is QuickConvert really free?

Yes. Every tool is free with no accounts, no downloads and no usage limits. The site is supported by advertising.

Do my inputs leave my device?

For most tools, no — conversion happens locally in your browser. The currency converter downloads public exchange rates and the QR generator loads a JavaScript library from a CDN, but your personal inputs are never sent to our servers.

Do I need to install anything?

No. All tools work directly in any modern web browser on phone, tablet or desktop.
