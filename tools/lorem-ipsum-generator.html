What is lorem ipsum?


Lorem ipsum is placeholder text used in design and publishing to fill layouts before the real copy is ready. Its famous opening — 'Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet…' — comes from a scrambled passage of Cicero's 'De finibus bonorum et malorum', a 45 BC treatise on ethics, mangled over centuries of typesetting.


Designers use it because it looks like real language: it has natural word lengths, sentence rhythms and paragraph shapes. This lets clients judge a layout's typography and spacing without being distracted by readable content — or worse, approving draft copy that was never meant to ship.


Use placeholder text early in mockups, wireframes and templates, then replace it with final copy before launch. Search engines and screen readers gain nothing from lorem ipsum, so never leave it in a published page — it also looks unprofessional if a visitor stumbles onto it.

Frequently asked questions




What does lorem ipsum actually mean?
Almost nothing — it is scrambled Latin derived from Cicero's writings. The words are chosen to mimic the rhythm and look of real prose without carrying meaning.


Why not just use real text as a placeholder?
Readable draft text distracts reviewers, who start editing words instead of judging the design. Meaningless placeholder keeps attention on layout, typography and spacing.


Can I use lorem ipsum on a live website?
You should not. It confuses visitors, hurts accessibility and looks unfinished. Replace all placeholder text with real copy before publishing.


How many paragraphs can I generate?
Between 1 and 20 paragraphs, each with 1 to 10 sentences — plenty for mockups, templates and design comps.
